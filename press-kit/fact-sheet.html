About the game

Quick Dungeon Crawler is a casual roguelite RPG built for short sessions. Pick a class, spend your stats, choose a passive and descend — then see how far you get before the dungeon kills you. Every run generates new floors, enemies and loot, and everything you carry out feeds the next attempt.

The whole game runs on the device: install it from Google Play or load it once in a browser and it keeps working offline. It is free, shows no ads and collects no personal data, and the full source is public under AGPL-3.0. It began as a fork of Red Pangilinan's Dungeon Crawler on Demand and is developed in the open by Werkstattl.

Key features

  • Endless randomized runs — new floors, enemies and loot.
  • Four classes — Knight, Paladin, Rogue and Scout.
  • The Forge — merge, reroll and lock equipment stats.
  • Companions — allies that fight and buff your run.
  • Curse levels 1–15 — optional endgame difficulty.
  • Bestiary — every monster tracked with kill counts.
  • Hardcore & Auto mode — real stakes, or hands-off.
  • Offline & installable — no connection, no account.
