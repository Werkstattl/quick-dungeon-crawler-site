Update 4.3.6 gives your dungeon achievements a place beneath your name, adds more control over Auto Mode and auto-selling, and takes a little of the sting out of enemy critical hits.

## Earn your title

You can now unlock **ten cosmetic player titles** by reaching deeper floors, unlocking Curse Levels, and defeating Dungeon Monarchs. Head to **Statistics → Titles** to choose the one you want to wear.

Your chosen title appears beneath your name and in run summaries. Earned titles stay with you across runs, so your next adventure can carry a reminder of what you've already achieved.

## Give Auto Mode a stopping point

Auto Mode now has a **floor limit**: set the floor where it should stop, and your run pauses when it reaches that point. The default is **Floor 20**. Choose a stopping point that suits your run so you can take over when you're ready.

## Auto-sell all rarities

The auto-sell rarity setting now includes an **All** option, letting you apply auto-selling to every rarity.

## Interface polish and balance

- Improved menu close buttons and the stat allocation layout.
- Updated translations across supported languages.
- Added a confirmation sound when loading more bestiary entries.
- Reduced the **enemy critical damage cap from 800% to 700%**.
