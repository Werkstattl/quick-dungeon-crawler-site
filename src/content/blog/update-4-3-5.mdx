Update 4.3.5 polishes how the Forge Member title is displayed and adds a home for the community.

## The Forge Member title, done properly

Until now, members saw their title appended to their name as `Name [Forge Member]`, which crowded the name line. Update 4.3.5 gives the title its own place: your name stays clean, and **Forge Member** appears as a gold subtitle directly beneath it — in the dungeon header, in the combat info panel, on the Statistics profile, and on the run summary at the end of every run. The title text follows your game language (for example *Schmiede-Mitglied* in German), and players without a membership see no change.

## Official Discord

Quick Dungeon Crawler now has an [official Discord server](https://discord.gg/U4DvQT3qfQ), linked from the game's [README](https://github.com/Werkstattl/quick-dungeon-crawler-rpg-od) alongside the subreddit — come say hi, share your best runs, and help shape future updates.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
