    Should I start in Hardcore?

    Normal mode is our recommendation for learning: you keep your inventory, equipped gear and gold after death. Hardcore is available from the start if you enjoy losing those resources when you die. Start in whichever mode matches the challenge you want, but back up your save before switching an existing character to Hardcore.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    How does Curse work?

    Curse is the dungeon's difficulty level. Higher Curse makes enemies tougher and raises the tier of regular equipment drops. Play your highest unlocked Curse to unlock the next one: reach Floor 10 for levels 2–10, then defeat the Dungeon Monarch for levels 11–15. You can choose a lower unlocked level when you want an easier run.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    What should I spend gold on?

    Gold pays for blessings, shrine healing, merchant offers and Forge actions. If you need Forge access, a wandering blacksmith on Floor 13 sells a Forge Token for 10,000 gold. Keep enough for the help your current run needs, then invest in equipment you expect to keep using. In normal mode, unused gold survives death.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    What is the difference between item tiers?

    Tier is tied to the Curse level of regular dungeon drops and affects an item's potential stats. A higher tier gives more potential, but item level, rarity, rolled attributes and refining also matter. Compare the actual bonuses for your build before replacing an item just because its tier is higher.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    How do I refine items?

    Open the Forge using the anvil icon, select Refine, then choose an item. Check the next-level preview and confirm with Refine Equipment. You need gold and Refine Stones, plus a Forge Token for the action if your Forge is not unlocked. Refining strengthens the item's existing stats and goes up to +10.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    What does Luck do?

    Luck increases the chance of an equipment drop after combat and helps you find companions. It does not directly improve equipment rarity or Refine Stone drop chances. For an ordinary enemy without an extra drop bonus, 50 Luck raises equipment drop chance from about 33% to 50%. The normal equipment drop roll is capped at 80%.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    How do I unlock companions permanently?

    Find Fairy Helper, Mini Dragon or Shadow Cat after combat to build that companion’s permanent unlock progress. Their targets are 100, 250 and 500 points respectively. Each find adds your current Curse level, capped at 10 points. Once unlocked, that companion joins your roster at level 1 in every new run. Wolf Pup is available from the start; Phoenix Chick has no permanent unlock in this version.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    Why does my attack stat not match the damage I deal?

    Attack is an input to damage, not the final hit. An ordinary attack starts at ATK × ATK / (ATK + target DEF), then receives random variation, critical-hit and other bonuses. If the target has as much defense as you have attack, the starting damage is half your attack. A dodged hit deals no damage and gives no Vampirism healing.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    Do level-up bonuses multiply my equipment stats?

    Health, attack and defense level-up bonuses apply to your base stats from allocation and class, with equipment added afterward. They do not multiply the equipment contribution. Stats such as Crit Rate and Vampirism instead gain percentage points. Compare the displayed improvement and your character caps when choosing an upgrade.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    Why can two items of the same tier and rarity have very different stats?

    Each item randomly chooses attributes from its equipment type’s pool, then rolls their values. Several rolls can stack on one attribute. Tier and level affect strength, rarity sets the base roll budget, and tiers 11–15 improve roll floors and percentage-stat limits. Matching tier and rarity does not guarantee matching stats.

    Read the full guide → Link to this answer
    Does a higher Curse or more Luck give better item rarities?

    Ordinary equipment uses the same rarity distribution at every Curse and Luck value. Curse raises the tier of regular drops; Luck raises the chance that an enemy drops equipment. Once an ordinary item is generated, Heirloom has a 1.2% rarity chance. Monarchs are an exception: their equipment reward is guaranteed Heirloom.
