Update 4.4.3 brings a small improvement for longer dungeon sessions: **the screen stays awake while you're playing**, so you can follow the action without repeatedly waking the display.

When you pause or leave the game, the normal screen timeout is restored.

Read the [full release notes on GitHub](https://github.com/Werkstattl/quick-dungeon-crawler-rpg-od/releases/tag/4.4.3).
