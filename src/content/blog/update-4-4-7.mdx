Update 4.4.7 gives defensive gear a new reason to keep diving: **eight special effects** that new armor, shields, and helmets can carry into every fight. It also turns battles into a torchlit crypt scene and gives character creation a proper facelift.

## New special effects on defensive gear

Every eligible **new drop or forged piece** has a 1 in 4 chance to roll one permanent special effect. The effect is shown directly on the item card, so you can spot it before you equip it. Existing gear does not gain effects retroactively, so keep an eye out in the dungeon.

**Armor**

- **Plate — Hit Recoil:** when an enemy damages you, it has a 4–12% chance to miss its next turn.
- **Chain — Critical Defense:** enemy critical hits deal 20–50% less bonus damage.
- **Leather — Second Wind:** when you dodge, recover 1–3% of your maximum HP.

**Shields**

- **Tower — Shield Block:** each incoming hit has an 8–20% chance to deal half damage.
- **Kite — Deflection:** when an enemy hits you, it takes 5–15% of that damage back.
- **Buckler — Riposte:** when you dodge, you have a 10–30% chance to counter for one basic attack's damage.

**Helms**

- **Great Helm — Last Stand:** while below 30% HP, take 10–25% less damage from enemy hits.
- **Horned Helm — Opening Strike:** your first hit of each fight deals 15–40% more damage.

All eight effects trigger inside the normal combat loop, so they work in every run and at every curse level.

## Torchlit crypt battles

Combat now has a real setting: enemies stand in a **torchlit crypt**, with a new backdrop behind the sprite, a soft shadow under their feet, and damage numbers floating over the scene. The scene scales down cleanly with the compact corner combat panel.

## Redesigned character creation

The "What is your name?" screen is now a themed card with the same torchlit crypt banner and a knight's-helm medallion, with cleaner spacing around the name field, the Hardcore option, and Confirm. Labels and validation messages were also cleaned up so screen readers announce them properly.
