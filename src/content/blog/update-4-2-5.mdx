Update 4.2.5 is a small patch with two changes.

## Dungeon Monarch gets its own Auto Mode toggle

Auto Mode now has a dedicated **Dungeon Monarch** checkbox in its settings, right below *Boss Doors*. Until now the monarch chamber followed the Boss Doors preference; with its own toggle you can let Auto Mode accept mysterious monarch chambers while deciding boss doors yourself — or the other way around. The toggle is on by default (same behavior as before for existing saves), and you can turn it off any time. Fully translated in all 31 languages.

## Cheaper stat-lock rerolls at high tiers

Locking stats during a Forge reroll now costs fewer **Refine Stones** on higher-tier gear — the per-tier scaling factor drops from 0.18 to 0.16. Concretely: a Heirloom tier 12 item with 3 locked stats goes from **149 to 138 stones**. Gold costs are completely unchanged.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
