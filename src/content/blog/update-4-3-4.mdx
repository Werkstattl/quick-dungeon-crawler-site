Update 4.3.4 gives Forge Members a pay-once option and adds a few quality-of-life touches.

## Lifetime Forge Membership

The Forge Membership now comes in two plans:

- **Lifetime Membership** — €10.59 + VAT, one-time purchase. All membership benefits forever, no renewal.
- **Monthly Membership** — now €1.09 + VAT per month (previously €0.99). Auto-renewing, cancelable anytime through your store.

Both plans keep the same benefits: access to all premium features, expanded inventory (+50 slots), enhanced resting recovery, +10% gold found, and the exclusive Forge Member title. Your existing monthly subscription is untouched — buying Lifetime does not cancel it, and lifetime access is restored automatically on all your devices, even offline.

## Companion EXP progress

The Companion panel now shows your active companion's experience as `EXP current / required`, so you can see exactly how close it is to the next level. The counter updates live during runs and hides itself when no companion is active.

## Cleaner dungeon HUD

The time, floor and room counters in the middle of the dungeon screen now wrap and scale properly on narrow phones instead of crowding the control buttons. Forge lists also gained an **Include refinement** toggle and clearer base-stat previews when rerolling.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
