Update 4.3.1 makes the game's rarest companion a permanent reward and fixes two rough edges.

## Permanent Phoenix unlock

The Companion roster has a new milestone: **Phoenix Chick**, the only findable Legendary companion (it evolves into the Phoenix at Lv.50), can now be unlocked permanently. Every Phoenix Chick you find during a run adds to your unlock progress, and the amount depends on your active **Curse level** — a find on Curse 10 is worth ten times as much as a find on Curse 1:

- **Progress needed:** 1,000
- **Progress per find:** equal to your Curse level, up to **+10** at Curse 10 or higher
- **Where to see it:** the Companion panel shows your current `Unlock: X/1000` progress under the Phoenix Chick card

Once you hit 1,000, the Phoenix Chick **joins every new run automatically** — no more relying on luck to recruit it. The same permanent-unlock system already covers the Fairy Helper (100), Mini Dragon (250) and Shadow Cat (500); the Phoenix is simply the top tier of that ladder.

## Buckler rarity colors

Bucklers shared the generic gray shield icon regardless of rarity. They now inherit their item's rarity color like all other gear — a Legendary Buckler finally looks Legendary in the inventory.

## Purchase system and Auto Mode fixes

Under the hood, the in-app purchase system moved to a new billing library. Alongside it, Auto Mode ownership detection was fixed: if you already own Auto Mode — through the one-time purchase, a Forge membership, or as a long-time player — the game now detects and keeps that access reliably.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
