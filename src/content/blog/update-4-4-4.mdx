Your level 100 gear has a new upgrade path. Update 4.4.4 introduces **Guardian Cores** for refining beyond +10, lets you spend gold on extra level-up rerolls, and expands the Floor 13 merchant's offer at higher curse levels.

## Take level 100 gear beyond +10

Equipment at **level 100** can now be refined past +10 in the Forge. These upgrades require Guardian Cores alongside gold and Refine Stones, with costs increasing as you refine further. Equipment below level 100 keeps its +10 refinement limit.

**Floor bosses can drop Guardian Cores from Floor 20 onward.** Drops are not guaranteed: your chances improve as you reach deeper floors, take on higher curse levels, and increase your Luck.

## Keep rerolling level-up choices

The two free level-up rerolls remain. Once you've used both, you can **pay gold for additional rerolls** to keep looking for the stats you want.

The price scales with your character level and selected curse level. Check the gold cost shown on the reroll button before spending.

## Two Forge Tokens at curse level 6 and above

At **curse level 6 or higher**, the wandering blacksmith on Floor 13 now offers **two Forge Tokens for 20,000 gold in total**. At lower curse levels, the offer remains one token for 10,000 gold.

## Run summary and balance changes

The run summary now includes **Refine Stones collected**, making it easier to see what you brought in during a dungeon run.

Enemy gold rewards and the cap on enemy experience rewards have also been slightly reduced.
