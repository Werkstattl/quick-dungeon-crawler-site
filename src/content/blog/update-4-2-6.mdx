Update 4.2.6 is a small quality-of-life patch for Auto Mode players.

## Auto Mode can equip your best gear

Auto Mode settings have a new **Equip best** checkbox. When enabled, every equipment drop is immediately compared against your loadout and equipped if it is better — no more pausing to swap gear mid-run. Two safeguards stay in place:

- **Locked gear stays equipped.** A locked item is never replaced by an auto-equip.
- **Auto-sell still comes first.** Items below your auto-sell rarity are sold before the equip check, so your settings keep working exactly as before.

The drop log still tells you what an enemy dropped, and the item's sell controls follow it to its new location (equipped slot or inventory). Fully translated in all 31 languages.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
