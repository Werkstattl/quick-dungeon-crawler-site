Update 4.4.1 gives you a combat screen that stops hiding the dungeon, sharpens the route choice screen, rebalances what Refining costs, and adds native review prompts plus Apple platform support.

## Keep an eye on the dungeon while you fight

A new **Combat panel in corner** setting is waiting in **Settings**. It is off by default, so nothing changes until you switch it on.

With it enabled, the fight moves into a compact card in the lower right corner instead of covering the screen. Your **stats panel, companion, the timer row and the exploration log stay visible** the whole fight, and the dungeon view is no longer dimmed behind the combat panel. You can also flip the setting during a fight and see it apply right away.

## Routes you can read at a glance

The four route choices — Balanced, Spoils, Sanctuary and Descent — now have their own icons and colours in a cleaner grid layout, so the floor you are about to walk into is easier to pick at a glance.

## Refine costs, rebalanced

Refining in the Forge now asks for **5× more Gold** per refinement (the minimum cost per step rises from 500 to 2,500 Gold), while **Refine Stone requirements are about 18% lower**: taking an item from +0 to +10 now costs **45 Refine Stones instead of 55**.

Per-step Stone costs now run 1, 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 6, 6, 7, 8 instead of 1 through 10, so the late refinements get cheaper while the Gold bill grows. Expect to spend Gold rather than farm Stones for your final upgrades.

## Review prompts that wait their turn

On Android and on Apple platforms the game now uses the store's native in-app review sheet. Prompts **wait until your level-up choices are done** and never interrupt combat, an open menu or another choice, or Auto Mode running unattended. On mobile the request is also capped to once every 120 days instead of once a week, and **rating the game no longer grants a level up** — that reward is gone entirely.

## iOS, iPadOS and macOS

Quick Dungeon Crawler is now available on the **App Store** for iPhone, iPad and Mac, and the in-game rating link points to the right store for your platform.

## Also in this update

- Translations were refreshed across all supported languages.
- Companion terminology is now consistent, including in the Auto Mode settings.
