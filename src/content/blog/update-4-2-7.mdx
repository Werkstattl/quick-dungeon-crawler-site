Update 4.2.7 makes the deep end of the dungeon pay off and clears up the Curse selection screen.

## Monarch spoils scale with depth

The Mysterious chamber and its Dungeon Monarch fight are no longer a flat reward. Every Monarch kill from **Floor 25** on now pays extra loot on top of the normal spoils, and it grows every 5 floors:

- **Floor 25:** +1 Heirloom item, +2 Refine Stones
- **Floor 30:** +2 Heirloom items, +4 Refine Stones
- **Floor 35:** +3 Heirloom items, +6 Refine Stones
- **Floor 40:** +4 Heirloom items, +8 Refine Stones
- **Floor 45 and deeper:** +5 Heirloom items, +10 Refine Stones (cap)

The chamber tells you the exact bonus before you walk in, so you always know what the fight is worth before committing.

## Curse selection, cleaned up

The Curse dropdown now shows **only unlocked Curse levels** — no more scrolling through rows of "(Locked)". Below the selector, a single hint tells you exactly how to unlock the next level, for example *"Reach Floor 10 on Curse Level 2 to unlock Curse Level 3."* or *"Defeat the Dungeon Monarch on Curse Level 10 to unlock Curse Level 11."*

## A warmer, more readable look

The interface got a full visual refresh: a warm dark background with parchment-toned text and gold accents, restyled buttons, inputs and dropdowns, a more visible header bar, and modals that fit the screen properly on mobile. New and changed text is fully translated in all 31 languages.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
