Update 3.9.1: Reroll Stats and Wandering Shop

May 29, 2026

Update 3.9.1 adds equipment stat rerolls, a wandering merchant event, and clearer Aegis Thorns combat feedback.

by 7underlines

developer @ Werkstattl

Update 3.9.1 expands The Forge and adds a new way to find valuable equipment during a run.

Highlights

  • Reroll Equipment Stats — Spend gold in The Forge to reroll an item’s stats. The item keeps its level, tier, rarity, and refinement level while receiving a new set of stats from its equipment-specific stat pool.
  • Wandering Shop — A wandering merchant now appears on floor 6. Spend gold for a guaranteed Rare-or-better equipment item, with the price scaling to your level and current Curse Level.
  • Aegis Thorns Feedback — Reflected damage from Aegis Thorns is now shown directly in the combat log.

The reroll screen shows the current stats and the possible stat types before you commit, making it easier to decide which equipment is worth investing in.

Thank you for continuing to share your feedback and builds!
