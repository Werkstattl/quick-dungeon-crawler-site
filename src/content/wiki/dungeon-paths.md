## What each path means

From Floor 2 onward, you choose a path at the start of each floor. Your choice changes the mix of exploration events for that floor.

| Path          | What to expect                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Balanced**  | The normal mix of everything.                                 |
| **Spoils**    | More treasure chests and fewer enemies.                       |
| **Sanctuary** | More shrines and even fewer enemies.                          |
| **Descent**   | A higher chance of quickly finding the door to the next room. |

Paths change the chances of encounters, so a particular event is never guaranteed by your path choice.
