Update 4.1.1 introduces Forge Tokens, more useful merchant offers, and several balance and reliability improvements.

## Forge Tokens on Floor 13

Players with a locked Forge can meet a Wandering Blacksmith once per run on Floor 13 and buy one **Forge Token for 10,000 Gold**.

A token grants access to one complete **Merge, Reroll, or Refine** action. The normal Gold cost still applies, and Refine continues to require Refine Stones. Players with the permanent Forge unlock or an active Forge Membership retain unlimited access and do not spend tokens.

## Wandering Merchant improvements

The Floor 6 Wandering Merchant now offers **Rare or better equipment**. With all six equipment slots filled, the offer scales to the equipped loadout while its tier follows the current Curse Level. Prices have also been adjusted to keep these stronger offers meaningful.

Auto Mode now purchases offers from both Wandering Merchants instead of automatically skipping them, provided the player has enough Gold.

## Balance changes

- Gold income and item sell values have been reduced by about **15%**.
- Blessing costs now increase by **5% for each Curse Level above 1**.
- The Enraged enemy modifier now grants **+30% Attack** instead of +40%, while its **-20% maximum HP** tradeoff remains unchanged.

## More improvements

- Added the official Discord community link to the game menu.
- Improved recovery from inconsistent saved combat states.
- Removed obsolete navigation from equipment comparisons.
