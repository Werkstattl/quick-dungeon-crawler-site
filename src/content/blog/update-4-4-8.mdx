Quick Dungeon Crawler now supports **36 language options**. You can choose Hungarian, Greek, Bulgarian, Marathi, or Latin American Spanish in Settings. Spanish from Spain is still available as a separate option.

## Also in this update

- **Easier stat allocation on small screens.** The stat-point panel during character creation makes better use of narrow screens, with compact Reset and Auto controls where space is tight.
- **More translated messages.** Companion attacks, level-ups, and evolutions now use your selected language, as does the warning when your inventory is full.
- **Save backup fix for older Android versions.** The backup code no longer relies on a JavaScript feature missing from some older Android WebViews.

Read the [full release notes on GitHub](https://github.com/Werkstattl/quick-dungeon-crawler-rpg-od/releases/tag/4.4.8).
