Update 4.4.1 introduces a new way to arrange combat, makes route choices easier to read, and adjusts equipment refining costs.

## Move combat into the corner

Enable **Combat panel in corner** in Settings to keep your player stats, companion information, and exploration log visible during fights. The compact combat panel sits in the corner while the rest of the dungeon view stays on screen.

Route selection also has an updated layout with distinct icons and colors to help you tell the choices apart.

## Refining costs have changed

Equipment refining now costs **five times as much gold as before**, while requiring **fewer Refine Stones**. Check the displayed costs when planning your next upgrade.

This change applies to refining. The adjustments to rerolls with locked stats were covered in [Update 4.4.0](/blog/update-4-4-0/).

## Quieter review requests

Android and Apple platforms now support native in-app review requests. Requests appear less frequently, wait until level-up choices are complete, and avoid interrupting combat, menus, or unattended Auto Mode. The level-up reward for rating the game has been removed.

The update also adds App Store links, refreshes platform information for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS, and improves translations and companion terminology.

Read the [full release notes on GitHub](https://github.com/Werkstattl/quick-dungeon-crawler-rpg-od/releases/tag/4.4.1).
