This post covers releases 4.3.8 and 4.3.9, picking up where Update 4.3.6 left off. The headline feature tells you how good a drop really is, and there are new Quality-of-Life options for Auto Mode, combat, and Curse selection.

## Judge any drop by roll quality

Every stat on an item is rolled randomly between a minimum and maximum value. **Show roll quality** in item details now shows exactly where your item lands: a percentage bar per stat, the number of rolls behind each stat, and a **How roll quality works** breakdown. For example, an item with two Attack rolls might read *ATK per roll: 29–168* and *This item: 140 × 2 = 280 ATK*, landing at 80% roll quality. Roll quality always uses base stats, and it works for equipment and companion charms alike.

## Roll quality in the Forge

The same quality indicators now appear alongside stat locks and in the reroll preview, so you can see at a glance which stats are worth keeping before you spend Gold and Refine Stones.

## Compare with or without refinement

Item details gained an **Include refinement** toggle so you can compare equipment with or without refinement bonuses. Roll quality itself always reflects the base item.

## New options for Auto Mode and combat

- **Stop when a new pet is found** - an optional Auto Mode setting that turns Auto Mode off and pauses your run when you find a new companion, so a pet discovery never slips by.
- **Hide Auto Attack** - removes the Auto Attack toggle from the combat screen if you never touch it.
- Fixed initialization of Auto Mode's floor limit when no limit was stored yet.

## What Curse levels actually do

The Curse level selection now has a **help icon** that explains the scale in one line: higher Curse levels make creatures stronger and reward you with better loot.

## Balance: Open Wounds

The Open Wounds bleed was retuned to reach its full effect faster:

- Damage per stack increased from **0.2% to 0.4%** of the target's max health per second.
- Maximum stacks reduced from **25 to 13**.

Full-strength bleed now deals 5.2% of max health per second after 13 stacks instead of 5.0% after 25, so stacks build up twice as fast. Translations were also updated across supported languages.
