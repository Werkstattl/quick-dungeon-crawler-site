Update 4.4.0 adjusts the cost of Forge rerolls with locked stats and makes equipment details easier to read.

## Adjusted costs for locked rerolls

Rerolling equipment in the Forge with stats locked now costs **slightly more gold**, while **Refine Stone costs are lower at higher item tiers**.

If you're working on higher-tier gear and keeping selected stats, check the updated costs before your next reroll.

## Equipment details stay on screen

The equipment info window now stays within the screen boundaries, keeping item details in view when you inspect your gear.
