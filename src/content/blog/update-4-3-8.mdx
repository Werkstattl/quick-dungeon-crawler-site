Update 4.3.8 makes it easier to judge your equipment and decide which stats to keep when rerolling.

## See how well each stat rolled

Enable **Show roll quality** in item details to see how close each stat is to its maximum possible roll. The display includes percentages, roll counts, and a breakdown of each stat's roll range. It works for both equipment and companion charms.

Quality indicators also appear beside stat locks in the Forge and in the reroll preview, so you can compare the rolls before deciding what to keep.

The new **Include refinement** toggle lets you compare item stats with or without refinement bonuses. Roll quality always uses the item's base stats.

## More control over Auto Mode

An optional setting now stops Auto Mode and pauses your run when you find a new companion. There's also a setting to hide the Auto Attack toggle from the combat screen.

This update also improves initialization of Auto Mode's floor limit and updates translations across supported languages.
