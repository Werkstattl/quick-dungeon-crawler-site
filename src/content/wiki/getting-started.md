## Your first run

1. **Create your hero.** Choose a class and passive that sound interesting. Leave Hardcore off if you want to build up a collection of equipment across attempts.
2. **Begin at Curse 1.** Higher Curse levels unlock as you progress. You can learn the combat and equipment systems before increasing the challenge.
3. **Explore and read the event log.** Encounters, loot and choices appear there. Watch your health, and use opportunities to recover when you need them.
4. **Check your equipment.** Fill empty slots and compare new items with what you already wear. An item's actual attributes matter as well as its colour.
5. **Choose level-up bonuses for the problem you face.** If fights are ending your run, consider health or defense; if you survive but struggle to finish enemies, consider offense.

Our beginner tip: keep your first few runs simple. Learn how equipment changes your character before spending heavily on upgrades or chasing a specialised build.

## Normal mode and Hardcore

Normal mode is also called **non-Hardcore** or **softcore** by players.

In both modes, death ends the current run. Your character level, experience progress, level-up bonuses and dungeon position reset. **Normal mode still gives you lasting progress through the inventory, equipment and gold you keep.**

| After death                         | Normal mode | Hardcore |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Character level and run bonuses     | Reset       | Reset    |
| Dungeon floor and room              | Reset       | Reset    |
| Gold                                | Kept        | Lost     |
| Inventory equipment and consumables | Kept        | Lost     |
| Equipped regular gear               | Kept        | Lost     |
| Refine Stones and Forge Tokens      | Kept        | Lost     |

**Our recommendation:** begin in normal mode if you are still learning what the systems do. Hardcore is a reasonable first choice if you specifically enjoy starting over after a defeat. You do not need to complete normal mode first.

Hardcore and [Curse](/wiki/curse/) are separate choices: Hardcore changes what you lose, while Curse changes the challenge of the dungeon and the tier of its loot.

## Switching to Hardcore

Enabling Hardcore for an existing character can immediately clear your existing items, equipped gear and gold. It is not a way to bring a normal-mode stockpile into a Hardcore run.

**Export your save before making the switch.** Read the in-game warning carefully. A later change of mode does not restore items that were already removed.

## Save a backup

Open the game menu and choose **Export/Import Data**. Copy the exported data and keep it somewhere safe outside the game. Use the import field when you want to restore that backup.

A backup captures your player data at a point in time. Importing it replaces your current player data and resets the dungeon run; it does not resume the room you exported from. Make a fresh export before restoring an older backup.
