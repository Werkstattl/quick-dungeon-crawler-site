Update 4.2.4 is a small quality-of-life patch with two changes.

## Help & Wiki in the menu

The in-game menu has a new **Help & Wiki** entry, right below *The Forge Membership*. One tap opens the [official wiki](https://quickdungeoncrawler.com/wiki/) in your browser — mechanics explanations, guides, and tips without leaving the game to search for them.

The entry is fully translated in all 31 languages, and like the Rate Game, Discord, and Subreddit buttons it opens as an external link.

## Forge header tidy-up

The sticky action row in the Forge header (**Forge Equipment** / **Clear Selection**) sat a few pixels too low when pinned while you scrolled long material lists. It now hugs the header edge cleanly — a visual fix only, no behavior changed.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged.
