A quality-of-life update for your dungeon routine. Update 4.4.5 shows where you left off before you jump back in, keeps your item selected in the Forge, and tracks Guardian Cores in the run summary.

## Know where you left off

The title screen now reads **Continue run** and shows your saved progress - **Floor 1 · Room 2** style, floor and room - so you can see where the run stopped before tapping back in. On a fresh save it still says "Tap to explore the dungeon".

## Guardian Cores in the run summary

The run summary now lists the **Guardian Cores** you collected during the run, right below Refine Stones, so you can see what a floor-guardian hunt actually brought in.

## Refine without re-selecting

After a refine, the Forge **keeps your item selected** and refreshes the preview immediately - refine again without picking the item a second time. Once an item can't be refined further, the Forge clears the selection as before.

## Auto Mode starts off on every new run

Auto Mode now begins **switched off** when a new run starts, instead of carrying over from your last run. Turn it back on any time in the dungeon.

## Guardian Cores in legendary gold

Guardian Cores now use the same **legendary gold** styling in the Forge counter and the refine cost line as they already had in drop messages.
