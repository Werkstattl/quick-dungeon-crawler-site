Update 4.3.9 strengthens each stack of Open Wounds and adds a little more guidance when choosing your Curse Level.

## Open Wounds builds up faster

Each Open Wounds stack now deals **0.4% of the target's maximum health per second**, up from 0.2%. The maximum number of stacks drops from **25 to 13**.

That means each stack does twice as much damage, with fewer stacks needed to reach the cap.

## Help with Curse Levels

A new help icon in Curse Level selection makes the explanation easier to find before you start your next run.
