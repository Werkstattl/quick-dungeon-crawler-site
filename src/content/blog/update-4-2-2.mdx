Update 4.2.2 gives the inventory a real cleanup tool and takes the unpredictability out of enemy crits: you can now sell loot in bulk by tier or level, and enemy critical damage follows one consistent rule from Curse 1 to the endgame.

## Bulk sell in the inventory

The sell dropdown in the inventory gained two new entries: **Below tier…** and **Below level…**

- Pick a threshold and the dialog previews the result before you commit — for example *"Sell 24 items for 169.86k gold?"* — so you always know what you are giving up.
- It sells everything **strictly below** the chosen tier or level, across all rarities.
- **Locked items and equipped gear are never touched**, so a stash clean-up can't eat your build or your keepsakes.
- The payout uses the same sale value as selling one item at a time.

## Enemy critical damage, rebalanced

At high Curse levels, enemy crits could spike to lethal amounts with no counterplay — some elites scaled their crit damage to well over 900%. This patch flattens that curve:

- Every enemy type now starts from the same **50% crit damage base**, instead of per-type values up to 900%.
- Crit damage scaling from Curse levels is **halved**.
- A hard cap of **800% crit damage** applies everywhere, including boss phases and older saves.
- Bosses no longer gain an extra crit damage multiplier **per phase**.
- Boss phase attack multiplier reduced from **1.25× to 1.20×**.
- The **Enraged** affix's attack bonus reduced from **+30% to +20%**.

Bosses and elites still hit hard — Enraged is still dangerous — but sudden one-shot crit spikes should be far rarer and much easier to read. This is a deliberate nerf to enemy burst damage; survivability at high Curse is now more about your stats and less about surviving a lucky roll.

## Fixes

- The level-up preview no longer shows negative values like `+-8%` when a companion bonus (for example the Phoenix's HP boost) is larger than the level-up gain — companion bonuses are now included in the preview.
- Auto Mode settings: the Blessings and Heal options now use their rarity colors.
- The combat panel got more height, removing scroll bars that appeared on some screens.

No save migration is needed — existing runs continue unchanged, and stored enemies are normalized to the new crit damage cap the first time they load.
